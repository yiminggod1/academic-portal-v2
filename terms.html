Terms of use.

Use Academic Library as an academic discovery and organization aid. The service does not replace the authoritative publisher or scholarly record.

Permitted use

You may use the public pages and browser-based research features for lawful research, education, writing and general information purposes.

External metadata

Search results and article records depend on external metadata services. Records may be incomplete, delayed, duplicated or corrected over time. External services and linked publishers have their own terms and policies.

Copyright and sources

Academic Library is designed to present bibliographic metadata and links rather than redistribute copyrighted papers. Respect the copyright, access conditions and terms of the original source when following external links.

Local research workspace

Saved records, history and notes are stored locally in the browser. Clearing browser storage, changing browsers or using a different device can remove that local information.

Changes and availability

The site may change, suspend or remove features without notice. No guarantee is made that every API request, record or linked source will remain available.
