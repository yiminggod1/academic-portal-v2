ABOUT THE SERVICE

How Academic Library works.

A lightweight academic discovery interface for reading, writing, and chemistry research. It does not host or redistribute copyrighted papers.

How it works

Your search is sent to public scholarly metadata services. The site normalizes titles, authors, publication dates, venues, DOI information, citation counts and available abstracts into a readable result format.

PRIVACY

Browser-first

Saved records, history and literature-review notes stay in this browser's local storage. There is no account or personal library database behind this static site.

SOURCES

Metadata, not copies

OpenAlex and Crossref provide bibliographic metadata. Follow the displayed DOI or source link when you need the authoritative record.

RESEARCH USE

Verify before citing

Metadata can be incomplete or change over time. Check the publisher, DOI or source record before using a result in formal academic work.
