ABOUT

A quiet layer between
question and source.

This site is a lightweight academic discovery interface for reading, writing, and chemistry research. It does not host or redistribute copyrighted papers.

How it works

Your search is sent to public scholarly metadata services. We normalize titles, authors, publication dates, venues, DOI information, and available abstracts into one readable result format.

PRIVACY

Browser-first.

Saved records stay in your browser's local storage. There is no account or personal library database in this static site.

SOURCES

Metadata, not copies.

OpenAlex and Crossref provide bibliographic metadata. Follow the displayed DOI or source link for the authoritative record.
