PRIVACY

Privacy policy.

This page explains what Academic Library stores in your browser and what can happen when third-party scholarly or advertising services are used.

Browser storage

Saved records, research history and literature-review notes are stored in your browser using local storage. They are not submitted to an Academic Library account because this site does not provide user accounts or a server-side personal library.

Scholarly services

Searches and live record lookups are sent to external scholarly metadata services, including OpenAlex and Crossref. Those services may receive standard technical request information required to provide their APIs. Follow their own privacy policies for details about their handling of data.

Advertising and cookies

When advertising services such as Google AdSense are enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar technologies in connection with ad serving. The site will disclose applicable advertising and consent requirements and will not ask users to interact with ads.

Google requires publishers using its products to maintain privacy disclosures describing relevant data collection, sharing and usage.
